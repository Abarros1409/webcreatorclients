How the score works. 35 points for a direct phone number (12 if there is only a website), 25 for how close the phone is to the decision-maker (independent 25 · small local group 14 · chain or hotel group 5), 20 for review rating and 20 for review volume — big enough to afford a site, small enough to care. Having a website is deliberately not scored; it only breaks ties, since a business without one is the easier sell.

Data. Ratings and review counts are the best public figure found per business (Google where available, otherwise RestaurantGuru, Tripadvisor, TheFork or Facebook — the source is shown on each card); 10-point scores are converted to a 5-point scale. Verify the phone number on the Maps listing before you dial.

Where your call log lives. When this page runs as a published artifact it keeps one shared record per business, so you and anyone you share it with see the same ticks and notes, updating live. Opened as a plain file it falls back to this browser's own storage — still saved between visits, but private to this device. The bar at the top always says which mode you are in. Either way, Export log writes a JSON file you can keep as a backup or hand to someone else, and Import log merges one back in, keeping whichever entry was edited last.
